Echo · tap swarm prototype

The legacy tap-swarm echo as an opt-in option for Echo: three shapes to judge by ear against Echo as it is and against the law ADR-142 rejected. Two slots, A and B, run side by side on the same source; the toggle crossfades between them in 20 ms, so both tails stay alive and the comparison is instant. The controls edit the slot you hear. Measurements, the cost and the poll: docs/design/tap-swarm.md. Keys: space toggles A/B, 1–5 pick the mode of the slot you hear.
