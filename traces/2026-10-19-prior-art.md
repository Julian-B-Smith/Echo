# prior-art — Phase 0 landscape written; design proposals D-004–D-011; name ruling D-012

- **Queue item:** ROADMAP T-001 (Phase 0 gate).
- **Why:** Phase 0 gates Phase A. The spec and the lab need the literature on interpolation, feedback stability,
  moving delay time, click and mono-fold measurement and IR display, and the competitive landscape. The name had to be
  checked before anyone designs around "Echo".
- **Method:** four research agents, pinned to Sonnet, read-only, web sources, in parallel. They covered (1) interpolation,
  feedback filters and failure modes; (2) repitch, crossfade, tempo change and click metrics; (3) ping-pong, mid/side,
  mono fold and IR visualisation; (4) public feature sets and the name collision. The lead synthesised their findings
  into `docs/prior-art.md` and checked them. The agents' raw notes were kept outside the tree (in the session group's
  shared notes folder), not committed.
- **Lead checks (verified by computation, not cited):**
  - linear-interpolation loss at 20 kHz and a half-sample offset: −16.7, −11.7, −2.0 and −0.47 dB at 44.1, 48, 96 and
    192 kHz;
  - 4-point Lagrange and Catmull-Rom have max |H| = 1.000 but sum|taps| = 1.25. This **corrects** an agent claim that
    they threaten loop stability (LIBRARY L0002);
  - the repitch, slew, critically-damped and tempo-ramp formulas (pitch 1 − dD/dt; peak rate |ΔD|/(eτ); +17 cents at
    D = 1 s under a 1 %/s ramp);
  - the mono-fold identity ρ = E_M / mean(E_L, E_R) = (1 + r)/2;
  - float32 position resolution of 0.25 sample at 2.3 M samples.
- **Evidence consulted:** README, ROADMAP (Phase 0, T-001), DECISIONS, CLAUDE.md §Domain, SPINUP-BRIEF.md,
  `intake/legacy/ADR-142-standard-delay.md`, and `intake/rack-slot-contract.md` (RS-3.6 equal-gain, RS-4.4 and §12 on
  the B265 click metric, A8), `intake/module-1.0-bar.md` (the B408 row). Fifty-three dated web references are listed
