# t041-speed-steps — T-041: the tape law's braking catch-up, the fade's outgoing head, raised-cosine time-fade weights, the gates at four rates

- **Queue item:** T-041 (D-049 A1, A3, A4); phase 1 (measure and prototype in a scratch copy), phase 2 (land, this entry).
- **Why:** three defects kept the click gates at 44.1 and 48 kHz and read in proportion to the sample rate. (1) The tape law's
  cap holds the head at speed 2.0 while the model runs ahead, and the deadbeat catch-up then lands it on the model in two
  samples (a step of 2.0 - s_model: the free scale sweep 4.08 at 48 kHz, 18.35 at 192; a `time.glide` change mid-move is the
  same kink). (2) `startFade` stops the outgoing head dead, so D-006's fallback, and a crossfade-mode retarget of a glide in
  flight, step a head at speed 2.0 or 0.5 to 1.0 in one sample (3.5 at 48 kHz, 14 at 192, both laws). (3) The time-fade weight
  is linear, so its slope kinks at both ends (the 3 ms jump: 1.9 / 3.6 / 7.0 at 48 / 96 / 192 kHz; no speed step in it).
  The human ruled B (braking catch-up), D (outgoing head slewed to rest) and raised-cosine weights, D-049 A4.
- **Evidence consulted:** CLAUDE.md §Domain; ROADMAP T-041 (criteria 1 - 6); D-049, A1 - A4; SPEC §3.5 - 3.7; `engine.h`
  (stepHead, startFade, tapeStep, readHead, backstop); `core_tests.cpp` (clickRun, clickCases, reversalRun, onsetClick,
  phaseSweepWorks, parallelFor); `lab/echo-lab.html` (phase 3: edited). Phase 1 ran in a scratch copy of `core/` (Release, outside the
  repo): a 145-case x 4-rate x 8-phase census, sample-level speed traces, nine prototype builds, a seeded stress run.
- **What changed:**

    - the tape branch of `stepHead`: while the head is off the model (|e| > 1e-9) its V aims at the model's slope plus the
      discrete stopping speed sqrt(a^2/4 + 2a|e|) - a/2 (never more than |e|), then the cap, then the bound
      V in [vPrev - a, vPrev + a] with `a = 1 / (k::kExpSlewS * sr_)`, the exponential law's bound (the constant is shared,
      its comment says so); on the model V is the law's own, bit for bit;

      fade sample and each after; the head is clamped inside the line (`kMinRead + |mod|` .. `lineLen - kKernelMargin - |mod|`);
      `vA` resets on landing (prepare, reset and a load's end land the heads; a Clear lands nothing, so its heads and their `vA` run on) and at the fade's end; nothing allocates;

- **Phase 4 (D-049 A6, the critic's FIX-THEN-SHIP):**
  - M1: the outgoing head's clamp is sign-aware, the form `modStep` uses: `clamp(dA + vA, kMinRead - offset, lineLen - kKernelMargin - offset)`;
    the offset is last sample's (`modStep` runs after the heads, moves it by at most 0.25 and holds the read itself under its own floor
    bound). The old `kMinRead + |offset|` form is the plant `fbClampUnsigned`. The "a guard, not a path" comment was untrue and is rewritten.
    The lab's `_slewOut` has the same form. Gate: new `outgoingBound` (row A11): the head's own speed (read position minus the offset)
    changes by at most `a` a sample across a crossfade retarget 1 - 12 ms into a 20 -> 2 ms glide under 20 ms of modulation at 1.25 Hz, both laws,
    four rates (worst 0.2268 / 0.2083 / 0.1042 / 0.0521 = a, at every rate; guard: at least 0.5 a). The old clamp reads 6.70 / 7.27 / 14.12 / 27.82
    (tape, retarget 12 ms in).
  - M1's M2 question: M2 DOES see the old clamp in that case (tape, 12 ms in: 24.7 at 48 kHz, 96 at 96, 379 at 192; exponential law unchanged), but a
    modulated case cannot be an M2 row at 96 and 192 kHz: with the clamp right it reads 2.28 / 4.55 (tape) and 2.29 / 4.57 (exponential), because the
    modulation's own offset slew (0.25 a sample, ending abruptly when the depth limit lifts after the retarget) steps the read speed, in proportion to the
    rate: not the fade's, and not T-041's. So `stepClick` does not carry it; `outgoingBound` does, rate-free. (Modulated glide only, no retarget: 0.59 / 0.67 /
    1.73 tape, 1.10 / 1.45 / 1.46 exponential at 48 / 96 / 192 kHz.)
  - M2 + M3: `catchUpRun` / `tapeCatchUp` gate by direction: shortening speed >= 0.9 and no overshoot of the model or target > 0.1 sample;
    lengthening speed <= 1.1 and target overshoot <= 0.1 sample, with no model check (during a lengthening cap the tape search only moves forward and pins the
    model's read to the head: `tapeModelDelay` = head delay + 1, e = -1). The engine comment and SPEC §3.6 now say the mechanism: there is no model to brake on;
    the target term is the brake.

    L2: the lab keeps its target term, with the parity reason and the < 1e-5 sample effect in its comment. L4: `catchUps` counts at most once a
