# m2 — moving time and modulation (ROADMAP M2, T-009)

- **Queue item:** T-009 (the M2 gate).
- **Why:** M2 replaces M1's declared placeholders with the spec's moving time and modulation. Before, repitch acted as a
  crossfade, every tempo move crossfaded, and `mod.*` was inert.
- **Evidence consulted:**
  - `spec/SPEC.md` §3.5–3.8, §7, §10, §11, §13, §15;
  - DECISIONS D-006–D-009, D-016;
  - the lab's `_headStep`, `_modStep` and `_tapeRebuild`, for intent only, never as a reference;
  - `traces/2026-10-20-m1-core.md`.
- **Built, in `core/include/echo/engine.h`:**
  - `stepHead`, with each move latched per §3.5;
  - the tape law: a varispeed model whose write position is recorded every 32 samples and interpolated quadratically;
  - the exponential law;
  - the 12 st cap, the 3 s fallback, and the speed backstop (`backstop`, counted, zero required);
  - the tempo modes: crossfade, glide, and follow with its 50-cent cap;
  - modulation (`modStep`/`modTargets`/`reseed`): sine and seeded mulberry32 drift, depth limited by slope and by each
    line's own heads, 0.25 sample/sample slew, a read-floor bound, 250 ms ramps, seeded switches.
- **Independent review:** an Opus critic subagent (fresh context, read-only) returned FIX-THEN-SHIP. All of it is fixed
  or recorded, and every fix is guarded by a check and a plant:
  - **H1, the tail cut a sounding glide:** D-025.
  - **H2, mod.seed:** an unchanged re-send reseeded, and a load ignored the seed (D-026).
  - **H3, the click gate met the letter only:** redesigned per D-023.
  - **H4, a tempo ramp hijacked a key move:** D-024.
  - **MED:**
    - follow never resumed after a step, and kept a residual (D-024);
    - reads went under the floor, uncounted (D-024);
    - the offset slewed in after a wake (§7 amended);
    - the tape history's error at fast glides (D-024, quadratic);
    - test gaps: modulation is now on in the block, determinism, sleep and no-alloc checks; the speed invariant covers
      all four modes; sample-rate independence of moving time is checked.
  - **LOW:** the keep-alive is deleted; values are cached; headers are updated.
  - **Recorded, not fixed:** T-008 (D-009's M1/M3 readings, and B265 beside them).
- **Two findings of my own, both test artefacts, both traced before deciding:**
  - The tape law's first tolerance was tighter than the 32-sample history the spec chose.
  - A click reading of 10.2 came from the probe's abrupt onset, which wrote a kink into the line (LIBRARY L0013).
- **Rulings by poll, 2026-10-21:** D-023 (click gate: s fixed at 2.25, one threshold 2.5, zero backstop hits), D-025
  (the tail clock restarts while a head moves), D-026 (seed changes only, and a load restarts the modulation).
  SPEC §7 and §10 are amended.
- **Tape law vs an independent full-rate model:** ≤ 0.008 samples and ≤ 2 cents of read speed at a 10 ms glide, 0.01
  cent at 0.3 s.
- **Memory, measured at prepare():** 18.71 MiB per instance at 192 kHz (lines 17.61, tape history 1.10), 4.68 at 48 kHz.
- **Verify:** `./verify fast` exit 0 before commit, with the core gate, the fp-contract probe, the plan map smoke and
  the lab smoke all green.
- **Open questions:** T-008; T-005 (the lab's rest gate); T-007.

## The 52 checks (echo_tests, all PASS)

- time accuracy: repeats on their sample; read centroid = delay (whole and fractional)
- generation ratio at 4 rates: gen2 = g(1+R)/2 exactly, then g within 1e-3 (S-1)
- ceiling law: identity to 0.9, then 0.9 + 0.1 tanh, on g·x (the returning term)
- ceiling: past unity the loop parks below 2.0 (input peak + 2 under input) and does not die
- dry path: at mix 0 the output is the input exactly, at 120 % feedback (ADR-142)
- mix 100 %: nothing before the first repeat (no cos(pi/2) leak)
- true bypass: settled-off filters are bit-identical whatever their cutoffs
- loop bound: measured |H_loop| <= 1 at 4 rates x 7 loop settings (D-005)
- sample-rate independence: time exact, loop filter within 0.5 dB at 44.1-192 kHz
- block-size independence: blocks 1, 7, 64, 333, 4096 and random render bit-identically
- determinism: interleaved instances = solo; reset() = fresh
- silence: exact zero in, exact zero out (9 routing x change modes at 120 %; awake engine)
- tail: exact zero from the declared tail on; kInfiniteTail at g >= 1
- tail formula: at 50 / 90 / 99 %, rest never cuts a sounding repeat (D-019)
- tail clock: lowering feedback or releasing a held loop never cuts the repeats (D-019)
- tail near unity: 99.99999999999999 % declares a huge or infinite tail and keeps sounding
- read across the line's wrap: a near-whole delay reads the right sample every lap
- FTZ is set inside process: a subnormal input is flushed
- rest never cuts a sound: the gate waits for the filters' ring below -120 dBFS
- non-finite input: repaired, counted (3 of 3), latched; the output guard too
- loop repair: a non-finite write clears all loop state; the wet recovers
- FTZ: the host's FP control word is restored
- real time: no allocation in process / setParam / reset / loadState
- I/O gain: unity at 0 dB; a 20 ms ramp linear in amplitude, at 48 and 96 kHz (B435)
- switches: routing and filter switches are click-free (20 ms equal-gain)
- crossfade: equal-gain; correlated heads never exceed the signal (D-007)
- jump: a 3 ms crossfade, never a bare jump (D-016)
- fade lengths: jump 3 ms, crossfade time.xfadeMs, at the rate (48 and 96 kHz)
- retrigger: latest target wins, intermediates dropped (SPEC §3.5)
- deferral: a time.change flip mid-fade changes nothing
- tempo: synced 1/4 at 60 bpm repeats at exactly 1 s from the first block, no glide in
- clamp: synced times fold by halving, free times clamp, 1 ms floor (SPEC §3.4)
- routing: ping-pong alternates; stereo never crosses; right wakes; M/S levels; M/S folds to M
- load: old repeats fade (not cut) and end within 20 ms; Device keys untouched (D-016)
- load: the old audio fades under its old settings, click-free (D-020)
- load: a second load mid-fade never restarts the fade (D-020)
- load: Device gain ramps run on through a load (RS-4.5)
- sleep: a slept instance equals its never-slept twin from the wake on
- parameters: unknown / non-finite / out-of-range / fractional values refused
- tape law = an independent full-rate tape model: <= 0.01 sample, <= 2.5 cents of speed (3 glides x 3 moves)
- exponential law = its ln-D recurrence; a big move runs at the 12 st cap
- repitch accuracy: output pitch = 1 - dD/dt within 1 cent from the move's start, both laws, fast and slow glides
- read speed in [0.25, 2.25], no reverse, backstop, overrun or floor: 4 modes x 5 moves x 3 mods; fallback past 3 s
- tail clock holds while a head moves: a 2900 -> 300 ms glide at fb 0 is never cut (D-025)
- mod.seed: an unchanged re-send changes nothing; a load derives the drift from the patch's seed (D-026)
- key moves keep time.change through a tempo ramp; follow resumes after a step and leaves no residual
- read floor under modulation (counted, zero); the offset lands on the curve at rest (no slew-in after a wake)
- moving time is sample-rate independent: both laws trace the same delay in seconds at 48 and 96 kHz
- modulation: depth exact, read = input at the moving position, slope limit, drift clamp, slew 0.25, spread 90 deg
- seeded modulation: reproducible from mod.seed; seed change and shape switch never step
- tempo modes: follow exact, glide by the law, crossfade never bends; 2x step crossfades; key moves obey time.change
- click metric (M2 gate, s fixed at 2.25): sweeps, tempo sweeps and single moves under 2.5 and inside the speed invariants; a planted click over
