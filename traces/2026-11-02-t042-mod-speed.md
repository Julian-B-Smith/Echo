# t042-mod-speed — T-042: the depth-20 report is real; the offset's slope bounded, the floor clamp answers to the sounding head, two floor brakes; the bar at every LFO phase

- **Queue item:** T-042 (D-049 A4, A7, A8, A9, A10); phase 1 (triage and prototypes in a scratch copy), phases 2 - 4 (land, critic, re-check).
- **Why:** T-041's stress run read a speed of -870 with reverse > 0 at `mod.depthMs` 20, and its modulated fade read M2 2.28 / 4.55 at
  96 / 192 kHz. Criterion 1: **the report is real, and it dates from M2.** `modStep`'s read-floor clamp bounded the offset by both heads'
  span, so a fade to a short time moved the offset by up to its whole size in one sample, and the outgoing head, still sounding, read
  the jump (5 ms sine at 1 Hz, 100 -> 2 ms crossfade at the trough, 48 kHz: 147 samples, speed -146, M2 33.9; reproduced by the lead
  with the engine's own `SpeedStats`). The backstop skips fades, so it never saw it. Worst M2 before: 105 / 124 / 495 / 1971 at
  44.1 / 48 / 96 / 192 kHz. Criterion 2: the offset's 0.25-a-sample cap starts and stops in one sample, a step of 0.25 sr per second,
  so 1.1 / 1.2 / 2.4 / 4.8 times the 10 000 per second bound; that reads 1.05 - 1.14 at 44.1 / 48 and 2.27 / 4.53 at 96 / 192.
- **Evidence consulted:** CLAUDE.md §Domain; ROADMAP T-042; D-049 A1 - A7; SPEC §3.6, §7, §13; `engine.h` (modStep, modTargets, headSpan,
  stepHead, outgoingSlew, backstop, readHead, pos, atRest, reset); `core_tests.cpp` (clickRun, stepClick, outgoingBound, speedInvariant,
  modulation, modSeeded, floorAndWake); `lab/echo-lab.html`. Scratch census: 252 cases x 4 rates x 8 phases, on six builds.
- **What changed:**
  - `core/include/echo/engine.h`:
    - `modStep`: the offset's slope changes by at most a = 1 / (k::kExpSlewS * sr) a sample and lands on its target by braking (A), keeping
      the 0.25 cap; on the curve the offset IS the curve, bit for bit (no lag);
    - during a fade the offset's clamp answers to the outgoing head alone (F); the incoming head rides the 3-sample floor via
      `pos(..., ride)`, uncounted, and only that read may ride; while the offset is under the incoming head's floor it aims at that floor,
      braking (W, the aim), and it leaves any bound from rest (W, the reset);
    - `stepHead`: a shortening head's closing speed toward the floor is limited to its stopping speed, allowing for the offset's slope (H);
    - `outgoingSlew`: the floor side of the clamp looks one sample ahead (D-049 A9 ruling 1); the ceiling side stays on `mods_[k]`, because
      5,568 directed runs never made it bind in either form, so a term there would have no reading and no plant;
    - `backstop`: a 1e-9 tolerance (0.5 + 0.25 sum to exactly 0.75, so rounding of about 3e-14 counted a hit); `modV_`, `modTp_`
      (`sizeof(Echo)` 2712 B, still "2.6 KiB").
  - `core/include/echo/plants.h`: `modAccelUnbounded`, `modFloorClampsBoth`, `modNoFloorBrake`, `modRideKink`, `modBoundKeepsSlope`, `fbClampStale`.
  - `core/tests/core_tests.cpp`: 34 modulated `stepClick` rows (eight gestures at the LFO's crest, trough and both crossings, plus two
    hand-placed: the aim at 1.3 s and the stale clamp at 1.38 s); `lfoLands` proves each row sits where its label says, the hand-placed
    two included; `speedStress` (3 configs x 4 seeds x 4 rates, reads `SpeedStats`, asserts the offset's peak so "depth 20" cannot go
    false again: at 7 Hz it is really 5.68 ms, hence the 1.25 Hz config); `outgoingBound`'s contact case at 2 a.
  - `spec/SPEC.md` §3.6, §7, header; `lab/echo-lab.html` (all of it, plus the core's ceiling clamp, which it never had);
    `tools/lab_smoke.mjs` (18g; the drift control now reads the offset's peak against the depth: the old form fired only because the lab
    had no ceiling); `README.md`, `CODEMAP.md`, `docs/admission.md`; CLAUDE.md's verify timing.

- **Side effects, stated:** H holds a deep dive back (100 -> 2 ms at the trough: D 564 vs 331 samples at +100 ms, the same by +200 ms; the exponential
  law's arrival slips from 291 ms to at most 354 ms at 192 kHz). At a fade's end the offset lands at the floor's bound in one sample
  (it can be hundreds of samples) while the incoming read stays continuous. After a depth ramp at 192 kHz the offset can leave its target by up to
  0.028 sample for 1 - 2 samples, then lands exactly. The floor brake is gated on the head's own speed, so where the offset closes on the floor and
  the head does not, the floor stops the read: 2.955 a, M2 2.16 (2.24 at worst), under the bar (D-049 A10).
- **Rulings:** four polls (A8: all four pieces with `stepHead` in scope, the tolerance, the lab, the gates); three (A9: every LFO phase, the bound as
  measured, the lab's ceiling); one (A10: the bound's sentence was still false, stated as measured, no engine change).
- **Critic:** FIX-THEN-SHIP twice. Round 1 found the stale clamp at the trough (M2 4.27, not a regression, gates only at the crest), an unguarded aim branch
  and exponential brake, a false "depth 20", and an overclaiming SPEC. Round 2 found no click over the bar, a read bound false on both halves, and a false
  claim in a lab comment. All landed; the third pass after that was small (a restored line, a comment, a lab control, two guards) and read by the lead, not re-reviewed.
- **Alternatives rejected:** A alone (leaves the jumps); A + F (8.6 at 192 kHz); capping the amplitude at D/4 (changes steady modulation at short delays, a
  SPEC change and no fix); gating the floor brake on `h.V + modV_` (only reaches 2.49 a, not clean).
