Q-A1 — the repitch law · ruled 2026-10-20: (d) tape and (c) exponential both ship, as the Repitch Law key

In repitch mode a moving time bends the pitch of what is in the line (pitch ratio = 1 − dD/dt). D-006 bounds the read speed (never backward) and asks which law moves the read head. Pick a move and a source, then switch candidates while it plays — the switch keeps the playhead, so you hear the same moment. The pitch trace below draws each law's glide. The two references are not candidates: crossfade mode, and the fixed-τ one-pole D-006 rejected (it clips at the hard ±24 st bound).
Trace: pitch of the sounding read head in semitones over the clip (gaps = crossfades). M2 (D-009): ≤ 1 means no discontinuity beyond what the pitch change explains; a speed kink reads a few, a bare jump reads tens. It is a measurement, not a pass/fail line — no perceptual threshold is claimed.

Q-A2 — synced time when the tempo changes · ruled 2026-10-20: (D), the Tempo Follow key

Synced time follows the host tempo (data, per block, interpolated per sample). Following a ramp bends the pitch by 1 + D·BPM′/BPM: a few cents at 0.25 s, a semitone or more at 3 s. D-008's candidates: (A) always crossfade to the new grid time, (B) follow through the repitch law, (C) follow exactly under a 50-cent cap and crossfade past it, and (D) make it a user choice — pick D if no single one is right for every patch. Note: horde's tempo rule says re-resolved synced values "glide through the parameters' own smoothers" (intake/tempo-divisions.md) — (B) is that rule; (A) and (C) would amend it, which horde would be told.

Q-A5 — what "jump" means · ruled 2026-10-20: a 3 ms crossfade

D-007 ratified a jump mode whose test asserts the click metric flags it: a bare jump of the read head. Drafting the spec found a conflict: horde admits no bare jump of signal-path state at a flip, and a morph of a continuous key must not step (contract A11, §12). A bare jump fails both. The alternative is a jump that is a very short crossfade (3 ms): no glide, no comb, no click. Hear them side by side.
